## Evidence classification

As artifacts are populated, each is placed in exactly one of these buckets:

- **Completed evidence** — present, anonymized, verifiable.
- **Evidence provided but awaiting review** — supplied and published, not yet reviewer-verified.
- **Missing evidence** — still `⛔ Awaiting project input`.
- **Planned future work** — future activity, not milestone evidence.
