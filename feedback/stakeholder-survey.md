# Stakeholder / Organizer Survey Instrument

> **Status:** 🟡 **Actual administered instrument.** The questions below are those actually used
> in the organizer/stakeholder feedback form, reconstructed from the response export. Only
> anonymized results are published.

**Audience:** Pilot organizers and institutional stakeholders (trainers, technical/support
staff, verifier operators, institution managers).
**Purpose:** Capture stage-by-stage ratings, observed problems, institutional value, and
readiness for a larger pilot.
**Consent gate:** used only where the respondent agreed their anonymous feedback could be
included in the pilot report.

## Questions

| # | Question | Response type |
|---|----------|---------------|
| 1 | Do you agree that your anonymous feedback may be used in the pilot report? | Yes / No |
| 2 | What was your role? | Role (e.g. Pilot organizer, Trainer, Technical/support staff, Verifier operator, Institution manager) |
| 3 | Please rate the following parts of the pilot (Planning and coordination, Participant onboarding, Credential issuance, Credential verification, Technical support) | Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor each |
| 4 | Which problems did you observe? | Multi-select / open *(organizer observation — not participant experience)* |
| 5 | How valuable is this solution to your institution? | 1–5 |
| 6 | What is its main institutional benefit? | Open (anonymized) |
| 7 | How ready is the solution for a larger pilot? | 1–5 *(mapped: 4–5→yes, 3→maybe, 1–2→no)* |
| 8 | Would your institution participate in another phase? | Yes / Maybe / No |
| 9 | What is the one most important improvement required? | Open (anonymized) |
| 10 | Any additional recommendation? | Open (anonymized) |

_Results and denominators are in [feedback-analysis.md](feedback-analysis.md)._
