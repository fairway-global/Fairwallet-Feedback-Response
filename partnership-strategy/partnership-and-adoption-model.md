## Partnership objectives

1. Integrate Fayda-based identity verification into the FairWallet credential-issuance process.
2. Establish formal pilot cooperation with Ethiopian employers and training institutions.
3. Test institution-supported credential issuance and presentation with real participants.
4. Develop a repeatable institutional onboarding and adoption model.

## Implementation activities

1. Fairway established formal pilot arrangements with IE Networks and Jasper Ethiopia.
2. Fairway requested and received approved access to the Fayda test API.
3. Fairway coordinated a FairWallet pilot on **22 May 2026** at **HUBBITS, Bole Bulbula, Addis
   Ababa**.
4. The pilot included **10 participants** associated with IE Networks and **15 participants**
   associated with Jasper Ethiopia.
5. Participants installed FairWallet, created KERI Autonomic Identifiers, and connected to the
   issuer.
6. Fayda test-API identity verification was incorporated into the credential-issuance workflow.
7. The IE Networks cohort used the **Graduate Trainee Program** credential schema.
8. The Jasper Ethiopia cohort used the **Fundamental Sales Technique** template (based on the
   **FaydaVerifiedAutoIssue** schema).
9. Participants received digital credentials and were guided through the credential-presentation
   process.
10. Participant and stakeholder feedback was collected or requested for evaluation and iteration.

> **Note on cohort numbers:** partnership records state **15** coordinated Jasper participants,
> while the [issuance register](../issuance-evidence/anonymized-issuance-register.md) evidences
> **13** issued credentials for that cohort. The 2-participant difference (coordinated but not
> issued/attended) is **to be confirmed**. See
> [participant coverage](../feedback/participant-coverage.md).
